```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: original illustration for a Nexus developer-console design preview, placed in the left 40% of a desktop login screen
Primary request: An original small chubby yellow-and-white orange tabby cat sitting on a tiny grassy rounded planet, gently looking at three small stars and a single moon, with one little flower nearby. The cat is the main focus and has a simple, lovable face and short legs. No human character.
Style/medium: restrained children's picture-book drawing made with colored pencils and selective dark ink outlines, with visible but very subtle hand-drawn texture and imperfect lines. Original character design. White/off-white paper feeling.
Composition/framing: vertical-friendly centered vignette with ample empty space around the scene, so it can sit beside real UI text. Keep the cat and planet fully visible.
Color palette: warm ivory paper, pale sage green, medium teal, muted golden yellow, soft terracotta-orange for cat accents, deep ink green outlines.
Constraints: no words, no letters, no logo, no UI, no watermark, no copied or recognizable storybook characters; low visual density, calm and friendly.
Avoid: photorealism, 3D, glossy effects, heavy gradients, dramatic shadows, crowded objects.
```
